FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Aryx hold my funds?

Never. Aryx connects through API keys with trading permissions only — withdrawal access is always disabled. Your capital stays in your own exchange or brokerage account at all times.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can run proven strategy templates out of the box or build your own with the visual, no-code indicator builder. Advanced users can still access the custom formula engine and API.

Which markets and brokers are supported?

Crypto (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit and more), US stocks & ETFs (Alpaca, Interactive Brokers), and precious metals via OANDA and MetaTrader 5. New integrations ship regularly.

Is automated trading risky?

All trading carries risk, and past performance never guarantees future results. Aryx is built to manage risk with hard limits, stop-losses, and circuit breakers — but you should only trade capital you can afford to lose.

Can I test before risking real money?

Yes. Every plan includes paper trading and a full backtesting lab, so you can validate a strategy on historical and live data before deploying a single dollar.

What does the free trial include?

14 days of full Pro access — all markets, all indicators, and the complete backtesting suite. No credit card required to start.
